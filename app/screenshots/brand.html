koppler
Local development · macOS and Linux

Run the part of your stack you need on your own machine.

Pick apps on a canvas and plug cables between them, then press Run. Everything you leave unplugged keeps calling the deployed environment you chose.

$ npm install -g koppler
$ koppler init
$ koppler canvas
