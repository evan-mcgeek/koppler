Databases on the board

One rule carries all ten: dashed means deployed, solid means on this Mac, for card edges and cables alike. Mono throughout; section 1 repeats in Paper as the light check, and the Scheme tweak flips the whole page.

1

Local never looks like Deployed: solid edge instead of dashed, "On this Mac" with a laptop, no environment tag, and a solid cable straight from the app.

7

PROD

A Deployed PROD database gets the PROD treatment on the card itself: a striped band and a 2px red edge, the PROD tag next to the switch, and a state line that never reads plain "Reachable". It always says "Asks before each run". Switching to Deployed on a PROD board asks first. A Local database on a PROD board stays an ordinary local card and says it isn’t PROD data.
