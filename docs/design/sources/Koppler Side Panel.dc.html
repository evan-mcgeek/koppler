Side panel: names that fit, and a width you can change

Default width 300. Kind in a 48px column, actions over the row on hover only, names on two lines at most. The same row is used by Koppler Areas, section 7.

The edge

The panel’s own hairline is the handle. It brightens to a 1px ink line on hover and focus, and to 2px while dragging. The hit area is 8px wide, centred on the line, so nothing is drawn to grab.

The canvas

The board reflows to what the panel leaves; its cards keep their places, so a wider panel covers more of the board’s left side until you pan. As the window narrows, the widest the panel can go shrinks so the board keeps at least 640px.
