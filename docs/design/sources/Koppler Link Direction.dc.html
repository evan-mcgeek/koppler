Which way a link goes

Plan, Live and Discover. One chevron pointing into whoever receives, plus a way to trace links on a board too busy to read.

1

One rule for every link

Every link with a direction reads the same way: it leaves the sender as a small dot and lands in the receiver’s socket ring, with one chevron just before the ring pointing in. Links that go both ways (databases, tunnels) have a dot at each end and no chevron.

Leaves
A small filled dot where the link leaves the sender.
Lands
One chevron, tip 6px clear of the socket ring, pointing in. The ring’s hole shows the last connection test where there is one.
Both ways
Databases and tunnels: a dot at each end, no chevron.
Do cables change?A little. The chevron from PR #569 gets the cable’s 2px weight and now appears on every link with a direction, app to app included. Every link starts with the same small dot and ends in the same ring, replacing today’s mix of end styles. Cables keep their colours, dashes and routes
SizeChevron 2px stroke, 10px across, tip 6px clear of the ring, in the link’s colour, dimmed and lit with it. Start dot 7px. Landing ring 12px, as in fix 12

Tracing on a complicated board

Direction alone doesn’t make a busy board traceable. Picking a card keeps its links and the cards on their other ends lit and dims everything else to 25%. Each lit link shows its verb. Hovering a single cable lights it and says in words who does what.
