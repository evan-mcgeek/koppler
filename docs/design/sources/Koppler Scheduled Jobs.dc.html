Scheduled jobs

A job is an app with a schedule, always the deployed one. It has a card in Discover only, beside the API it shares a repo with. Plan and Live don’t draw it: they say when a job shares data with what you run, and list jobs in the Apps list. Acme Shop: Nightly settlement and Fraud check, both from Orders API.

1

How a job reads as a job, in Discover

The schedule is the job’s second line, in words, with a clock glyph: the glyph is how you spot a job across a board; the words say when. The expression is in the tooltip, for the board’s environment. The kind tag stays the app’s kind; "from Orders API" says whose repo it ships from.

Not in Plan or Live

A job has no card on a Plan or Live board. Koppler never runs it and nothing tests it, and its calls go from deployed to deployed, so no cable from it touches what runs on this Mac. What does reach you is shared data: when a local app uses a deployed database a job also uses, the app’s card says so in one muted line. Clicking the line opens Discover with the jobs picked.

The zoomed-out card
